ONE SMALL DISCOVERY A DAY

How to play

Tap or swipe the letter wheel to make words that fit the crossword. A selected set of puzzles features at least three Scripture-linked words. Tap the small Bible words label to view optional clues. Remaining answers are ordinary words made from the same letters. Tap ENTER to submit a tapped word, or release a swipe to submit it. Each day has two free letter hints.

Finish the board to collect one Discovery Stamp and a Bible thought. Tap your stamp collection to revisit saved thoughts in your Bible Discovery Journal. You can replay anytime—no duplicate stamp and no penalty for missing a day.

Daily challenges now rotate 17 Bible-rich crosswords selected from the original 39; other levels remain available in regular Word Trails. The saved stamp records for earlier days remain unchanged. Daily progress stays separate from regular Word Trails, points and Insight Tokens.

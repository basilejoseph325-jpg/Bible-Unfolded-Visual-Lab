## Editorial rules for this and future batches

Each entry starts with a documented NWT 2013 / JW.org detail that is genuinely interesting in its own right. The discovery body should feel like a knowledgeable friend speaking: clear nouns, short sentences, a real setting, no academic jargon, no made-up emotions or motivations, no generic motivational filler. The final reflection must be thoughtful without implying a single mandatory decision. The **Share the Thought** material is an optional, respectful conversation opener, not a rehearsed sales script. No other denominational sources, invented quotes, cross iconography or Bible trivia are introduced.
